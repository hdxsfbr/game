# Playtest 2026-11-03: ranked enhancement list

Andre played the 3D build end to end on a desktop and sent fifteen notes. Each one below is traced to the
code that produces it, with a proposed fix and a rough effort (S: under half a day, M: a day, L: two days
or more). Ranked by how much each changes the experience for a first-time player, with quick wins pulled up.
Screenshots are in `scratch/playtest/` (not committed; `scripts/playshots.sh` regenerates them).

## Tier 1: fixes that block or confuse a first play

### 1. Chords smear and distort after the first one (S)
Reported at the Harmony Bench page one ("hear all four, in a row") and at the Jazz Quarter doors.
Cause, in `src/audio/synth.ts` and `src/scenes3d/World.ts` (`playDemo`):
- The `pad` timbre has a 0.5 s release and a 0.25 s attack. The chords are 1.1 s apart with a 1.045 s
  duration, so each chord is still ringing half a second into the next one while the next one is still
  fading in. At every transition eight notes sound at once, and neighbouring qualities clash by a
  semitone (E against E flat, B against B flat). The first chord has no predecessor, so it is the only
  clean one.
- Each pad note is three sawtooth oscillators detuned by 7 cents, so a four-note chord is twelve
  oscillators beating against each other: the "phaser" Andre heard. The 1200 Hz low-pass then removes
  the upper harmonics that separate the chord tones, which is why the inner voices blur together.
- Not clipping: measured in headless Chrome with an analyser on the output bus, the four-chord demo peaks
  at 0.64 of full scale.
Fix: a dedicated `chord` voice (one triangle plus one soft saw per note, no detune, attack 0.04 s, release
0.12 s, cutoff 3500 Hz), a 0.25 s rest between chords in the lesson demo and the door prompts, and the
bass note an octave lower and quieter. One code path serves the Harmony Bench, the three chord-tone doors,
the Band Stand and the Last Set, so one fix covers all chord sounds.

### 2. Melody doors replay only part of the tune (S)
Mayor's Choir and The Song. In `src/scenes3d/prompts.ts` `playDegreePrompt`: mid-tune with no miss, Space
returns without playing anything; after a miss it plays the tune only up to the current note. And every
solved note is echoed as a pad (`onStepSolved`), which reads as "single notes".
Fix: Space always plays the cadence and the whole melody, the status keeps saying which note is expected,
and the solved-note echo becomes a short confirmation click or is dropped during melodies. Also applies to
the Rehearsal Bench drill.

### 3. Doors are hard to spot, and opened ones leave no trace (M)
Closed doors are a slab in the wall's own frame colour, 1.35 units tall, with one small emissive knob
(`scripts/blender/build_area.py` `build_doors`). Nothing moves or glows until the player is within 2.2
tiles. Solved doors sink out of sight (`World.onSolved`), so the player cannot tell where they have been.
Fix:
- Closed: an emissive rim or glow strip in the area accent, a slow pulse driven from `World.update`, and
  a faint particle or "hum" ring on the ground. Keep the bubble.
- Opened: the slab swings or sinks but the frame stays and gets a lit lintel (accent emission) plus a small
  plaque, and Space still works there ("Sing it again": replays the puzzle without changing progress).
- Practice benches get the same closed/opened treatment once a drill has been completed (new progress flag
  `drilled`).
Screenshot `hub-door.png`: the player three tiles from the Hearth Door, which is not findable in the frame.

### 4. The tree doors in the valley look like scenery (M)
The Bright Tree and The Shaded Tree use `texture: 'tile-tree'`, built as an ordinary tree at 1.15 scale
inside two rows of trees, flanked by the valley's standing stones. Nothing says "this blocks the path and
can be challenged". Screenshot `valley-tree-doors.png`.
Fix: give both a visible barrier form: a woven gate of branches between two trunks across the gap, or a
glowing tree with an arch hollowed through it, in the accent colour, plus the item 3 glow. Drop the standing
stones there or move them to frame the gate. Generator only, no gameplay code.

### 5. The Bridge of Echoes door faces the wrong way (S)
The door tile is on the bank at the end of the bridge (`VALLEY` row 16, x 38), which is fine, but the
generator chose its facing by looking for walls beside it, and river tiles did not count, so it fell back
to facing north and south: side-on to the bridge (`valley-bridge-door.png`, Andre's first attachment).
Fix: water and trees count as impassable when choosing a door's facing (`door_axis` in
`scripts/blender/build_area.py`). Same rule gives the tree doors their facing.

### 6. Chord-tone prompt wording is ambiguous (S)
"Your turn: major seventh, the third" (Andre's fourth attachment) reads as two options. The label comes from
`CHORD_TONES` labelling in `src/world/puzzles.ts` and `listeningStatus` in `prompts.ts`. Fix: "This chord is a
major seventh. Sing its third." and the hint ladder marks the chord tones with 1, 3, 5, 7 from the first
listen rather than from hint level 2.

## Tier 2: gameplay shape

### 7. Interval doors: three tries before the answer is given (M)
Today a single miss replays the prompt as an echo with the answer in it (`playPrompt`, `slow`), so the second
try is a copy, not a guess. Proposed rule for produce-mode doors (fifth, fourth, thirds, serpent):
- Up to three attempts hearing only the reference (replay stays reference-only).
- Each miss costs one note of voice, as now.
- The answer is played only after the third miss, and the ladder marks it after the fourth.
- Score per door: 3 for first try, 2 for second, 1 for third, 0 after the answer was heard. Shown as
  stars on the map and summed on the results screen in place of the miss count medals.
Touches `PuzzleRunner.hintLevel`, `playPrompt`, `Results.ts`, `state.ts` (new `scores` field).

### 8. Jazz Quarter order and a new solfège door (M)
The quarter opens with chord-tone singing (Club Door, Alley, Cellar) and only then naming (Band Stand). Naming
a quality is the easier skill, and it is what the Harmony Bench teaches. Proposed order:
1. The Club Door: name the quality (random chords, 4 of them), the current Band Stand puzzle.
2. The Alley: sing the chord, one tone at a time, 1 3 5 7, for two qualities (new step kind `arpeggio`,
   a melody over chord tones with the chord held under it).
3. The Cellar: sing the third (major vs minor seventh).
4. The Band Stand: sing the seventh (dominant vs major seventh) and the fifth (minor vs half-diminished).
5. The Last Set: unchanged.
`src/world/areas.ts` plus one new step kind in `puzzles.ts`, `prompts.ts`, `puzzleRunner.ts`.

### 9. The map should show done and still to do (S)
Solved doors are drawn as a thin outline without a label (`MapOverlay.ts`); unsolved ones as filled dots with
titles. It is legible once you know the rule, but nothing says it. Fix: solved doors keep their title in a
muted colour with a check mark, unsolved ones get a small "to do" tag, and the area strip shows "3 of 7"
with a progress bar. Screenshot `town-map-partial.png` shows the current state with three of seven open.

### 10. A moment when a door opens (M)
Today: the slab sinks over 0.7 s, a chord and an arpeggio play, colour blooms outward, a toast says "The
way opens." Andre wants more. Proposed: a one-second camera push toward the door, a burst of accent
particles from the frame, the singer's "sing" clip held while the arpeggio plays, and the area's music layer
swelling for two bars before settling. Reuse the bloom; add a `ParticleSystem` and a camera dolly in
`World`.

### 11. Finale celebration on the Silent Stage (L)
`finishKingdom` plays the song with all layers, blooms colour and fades to the results after about nine
seconds. Proposed: the stage lights come on one by one in time with the bars, the curtains part, the
singer walks to centre stage, fireworks over the stage (particles plus bloom), the kingdom's four themes
quoted in turn, confetti on the results screen, and the results page itself becomes a credits roll with the
medal rows animating in. Needs a few new generator pieces (lamps, curtain hinges) and a scripted sequence in
`World`.

### 12. "Here Comes the Bride" demo is too short (S)
`lessons.ts`, valley page four: four notes. Extend to the first full phrase (eight bars worth of the
opening: 1 4 4 4, 1 5 3 4) so the fourth is heard in context. Also check Amazing Grace is mentioned but not
demoed; add it as a second demo button.

## Tier 3: hold for after the above

### 13. Camera (M to L)
The camera is a fixed-angle follow (`CAM_OFFSET` 7.6 up, 9.2 back, `placeCamera`). Common patterns for a
tile explorer are: this one, with a gentle look-ahead in the walking direction and a slow pull-back on open
ground; or a free-orbit camera on the right stick or the mouse. The look-ahead and pull-back are small
changes in `placeCamera` and would make corridors feel less cramped. A full orbit camera changes how the
door bubbles, the bench, and the glb's camera-facing facades were designed, so it is a larger job. Andre
agreed this can wait until the list above is done.

## Done on 2026-11-03 (Tier 1)
- Chord voice: a `chord` timbre (one triangle per note, 0.12 s release) replaces the pad for every block
  chord the player has to judge: the lesson demos, cadences, the chord-tone and naming prompts, the ii V.
  Lesson demos leave a 0.35 s rest between chords; the bass note sits an octave lower and quieter.
- Melody doors: Space plays the key and the whole tune, with the status naming the note you are on; a
  miss replays the whole tune slower; solved notes are no longer echoed one at a time.
- Chord-tone wording: "That chord is a major seventh. Sing its third, or find it on the keys, then Enter."
- Door facing: water and trees count as impassable when a door picks its facing.
- Closed doors: every door slab carries a glowing strip in the area accent (`door-glow`), kept in colour in
  grey areas and pulsed by the world. The tree doors are now gates of woven branches between two trunks,
  with glowing rails; their standing stones are gone.
- Opened doors leave a lit ring on the ground, and Space at one sings it again with no voice at stake.
  A bench whose drill has been finished gets the same ring and a tick on the map.
Screenshots after: `scratch/playtest-after/`.
